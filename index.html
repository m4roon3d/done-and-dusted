Bring your Google Keep notes in

Drop your Keep export here. Labels become categories; checklists become separate tasks; dates in the text become due dates.

  1. Open takeout.google.com, click Deselect all, tick only Keep.
  2. Create the export and download the .zip Google emails you.
  3. Upload the .zip here as is. Single .json or .html notes work too.

Or paste notes one task per line · a line ending in ":" starts a category
